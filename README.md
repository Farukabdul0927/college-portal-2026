# 🎓 College Portal 2026

A modern, web-based **College Management Portal** developed using **Python Flask**, **SQLite**, **HTML**, **CSS**, and **JavaScript**.

The system provides separate portals for **Students, Teachers, and Administrators**, allowing college-related academic activities and information to be managed through a centralized platform.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The **College Portal 2026** is designed to simplify and digitize common college activities such as:

* Student attendance management
* Marks and results management
* Study materials
* Assignments
* College notices
* Timetables
* Student profiles
* Teacher management
* Student management
* Administrative management

The project provides different dashboards based on the user's role and helps reduce manual work involved in managing academic information.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of the College Portal are:

1. To provide a centralized platform for college academic activities.
2. To reduce manual record maintenance.
3. To provide students with easy access to academic information.
4. To help teachers manage attendance, marks, assignments, notices, and study materials.
5. To provide administrators with centralized control over portal data.
6. To improve communication between students, teachers, and administration.
7. To create a simple, professional, and user-friendly college management system.

# 👥 User Roles

The application contains three major user roles.

### 👨‍🎓 Student Portal

Students can access:

* Student Profile
* Attendance
* Marks
* Results
* Timetable
* Assignments
* College Notices
* Study Materials
* Academic information

---

### 👨‍🏫 Teacher Portal

Teachers can manage:

* Student attendance
* Student marks
* Results
* Assignments
* Notices
* Study materials
* Timetables
* Academic information

Teachers can also work with student records based on their assigned classes and subjects.
